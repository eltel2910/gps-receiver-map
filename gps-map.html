Connection

Click Connect GPS, then choose the receiver's COM4 entry in the browser serial picker. The baud rate must match the receiver; 9600 is the common u-blox default.

On iPad or Android, the browser will ask for location permission. Open the app over HTTPS for device GPS access.

Use Device GPS on iPad or Android. Chrome or Edge on Windows is required for a USB receiver through Web Serial.
